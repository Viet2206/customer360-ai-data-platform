# Pharmacy benefits and preventive care

Medical and pharmacy benefits can have different networks, deductibles, accumulators, and authorization rules. The member's current plan documents and formulary control.

## Formulary and drug tiers

A formulary is the plan's covered-drug list. Drugs are often grouped into tiers such as generic, preferred brand, non-preferred brand, and specialty. Tier placement affects copayment or coinsurance. Coverage may depend on the exact drug, strength, form, quantity, diagnosis, and dispensing pharmacy. Formularies can change, so verify the version effective for the service date.

## Pharmacy utilization controls

Prior authorization may require clinical information before coverage. Step therapy may require trying another treatment first. Quantity limits restrict the covered amount over a period. A specialty drug may need a designated pharmacy or site of care. When a request is denied, the notice should identify the reason and available exception or appeal process.

## Refill and transition workflow

Before a refill, confirm remaining refills, timing, pharmacy network status, and whether the authorization is expiring. When coverage changes, ask about transition fills or continuity options before medication runs out. For an urgent clinical need, contact the prescriber and plan promptly; the search assistant is not a clinical decision-maker.

## Preventive services

Most Marketplace plans must cover specified preventive services without copayment or coinsurance when eligibility and in-network requirements are met, even before the deductible. Coverage is not automatically free for every visit involving a preventive topic. Diagnostic services, additional procedures, out-of-network care, or services outside applicable recommendations can generate cost sharing.

## Essential health benefits

Marketplace plans cover ten broad essential health benefit categories, including prescription drugs, emergency services, hospitalization, maternity and newborn care, mental health and substance-use services, laboratory services, rehabilitation, preventive and wellness services, and pediatric services. Specific covered services and cost sharing vary by plan and state requirements.
